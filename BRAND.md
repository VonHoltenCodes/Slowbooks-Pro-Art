# The SlowBooks Pro look

Use this for anything you make. When in doubt, ask in an idea issue.

## The idea

SlowBooks Pro is bookkeeping done carefully. The turtle is the point: slow and steady, unbothered, gets there, gets it right. The tone is calm, dry, confident, a little wry. Never frantic, never shouting, never salesy. The product is **free and source-available** (not "open source"; the licence is its own). Say "free", say "yours to keep", say "no subscription". Do not say "open source".

## The characters

They are the two from Aesop's fable, *The Tortoise and the Hare*. That is the whole brief.

**The turtle** is the tortoise who wins. Slow, steady, careful, unhurried, and he gets there. Draw him your way: your line, your proportions, your face. The house version today is a green turtle with a gold visor and a ledger, standing upright, seen in the product's release notes and on slowbookspro.com; match its spirit, not its outlines. Expressions we use: happy, focused, smug, surprised, wink. Poses we use: standing, pointing, peeking, celebrating, running (slowly), and a construction hard hat for the construction edition. The turtle appears in empty states, onboarding, release notes, docs and social. **The turtle never appears on anything a customer or a tax authority receives:** no invoices, no statements, no tax forms. A reference sheet goes in `art/mascot/` once one is final.

**The Hare** is the rival from the same fable, a rabbit: fast, flashy, forgetful, always rushing off somewhere, asleep under a tree when it counts. The Hare is the only competitor who ever appears, and only as the Hare. **Never name, show or hint at any real product.** No logos, no colour schemes that mean someone else, no "you know who". The joke is the fable, not a company.

The headline pair is **navy `#003366` on gold `#cc9933`** or the reverse. Green is the turtle's. Keep text at 4.5:1 contrast or better against its background.

## Type

The website and social art use **Inter**. Headlines bold, body regular. Numbers in a tabular figure setting where they line up (a ledger look). Do not set the wordmark in another face; use the logo files when they are published here, and the plain wordmark "SlowBooks Pro" in Inter Bold until then.

## Hard rules

1. Turtle theme. Always.
2. The Hare is the only rival, and no real product is ever named, shown or hinted at.
3. Nothing vulgar, hateful, sexual, demeaning, political or religious.
4. No real books: no real customer, company, amount or person. Obviously-made-up sample data only.
5. No build numbers, version strings or internal names in marketing art; "SlowBooks Pro" and, where it matters, the release number ("2.30") are enough.
6. Claims about the product must be true of the product as shipped. Ask if unsure.
7. Links point to slowbookspro.com. Nowhere else.
8. The turtle never appears on a document a customer or a tax authority receives.
