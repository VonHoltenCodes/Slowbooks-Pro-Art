# Code of conduct

This is a small, friendly place where people make art for a bookkeeping turtle. Keep it that way.

**Instant ban, no appeal, the content removed:** anything vulgar, hateful, sexual, demeaning or threatening, in a submission, a comment or a profile; anything that targets a real person; passing off someone else's work as your own.

**Be straight with each other.** Feedback is about the piece, not the person. "The green reads as neon on a phone" is feedback. "This is garbage" is not, and gets a warning.

**No spam, no self-promotion beyond your credit line,** no recruiting, no selling.

**Maintainers decide.** What is used, what needs changes, what is declined, and who is banned. We explain our decisions in a line; we do not argue them.

Report a problem to support@slowbookspro.com. Say which issue or comment, and we will look the same day.
