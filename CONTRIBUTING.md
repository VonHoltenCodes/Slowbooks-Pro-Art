# Contributing art

Read [README.md](README.md) first for the deal, and [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md) for the terms. Contributing means you accept them.

## The path of a piece

1. **An idea, if you want a steer first.** Open an [idea issue](https://github.com/VonHoltenCodes/Slowbooks-Pro-Art/issues/new?template=idea.yml): a sentence or two, a sketch if you have one. We answer with "yes, go", "yes, but …" or "not for us". Two minutes now saves a weekend later.
2. **A submission.** Open an [art submission](https://github.com/VonHoltenCodes/Slowbooks-Pro-Art/issues/new?template=art-submission.yml). Pick the avenue, describe the piece, attach the files (or link to them when they are large), say how you want to be credited, and answer the three questions about origin. One piece or one set per issue.
3. **Review.** We look at every submission. The possible answers:
   - `accepted` — we move it into the repo (a pull request, by us or by you) and it is ready to be used.
   - `needs-changes` — we would use it with an adjustment, and the comment says what: a size, a colour, a crop, a pose, a word. Reply on the issue or push to the pull request.
   - `declined` — not for us this time, and we say why in a line. Thank you all the same.
4. **Use.** When a piece ships or is posted, the issue gets `used-in-app`, `used-in-marketing` or both, with a link to where.
5. **Credit.** `featured` means you are in [ARTISTS.md](ARTISTS.md), the app's About, the website's credits, and the posts your piece is in.

Pull requests straight in, without an issue, are fine too; the submission form's questions go in the pull request's description instead.

## Files

- **Vector where it exists:** SVG, or an AI/EPS/PDF export. Keep text as outlines or tell us the font.
- **Raster:** PNG with transparency where it matters; JPEG only for photographs. Lossless originals, not screenshots of them.
- **Video:** MP4, H.264, AAC audio, 1080p or better. Project files are welcome but not required.
- **Size:** GitHub takes files up to 25 MB in an issue and 100 MB in a pull request. Anything bigger, link it (an unlisted YouTube video, a shared folder) and we will fetch it.
- **Names:** `area/what-it-is-vN.ext`, lower case, hyphens. `mascot/turtle-waving-v2.svg`, `app-icons/turtle-icon-1024.png`, `social/release-2-30-square.png`.
- **Where:** the `art/` folders match the avenues. If unsure, pick the nearest and we will move it.

## What every piece needs to be

- **Turtle-themed**, in the SlowBooks Pro look. [BRAND.md](BRAND.md) has the palette, the mascot and the tone.
- **Clean.** Nothing vulgar, hateful, sexual or demeaning. Nothing that mocks a real person. No politics, no religion.
- **Honest about the product.** A claim in a video or a post has to be true of SlowBooks Pro as it ships. If you are not sure, ask; we will tell you what it does.
- **Yours.** Your own work, or work you hold every right to. No one else's logo, character, photo, font or music without rights that cover us.
- **Free of the Hare's maker.** The Hare, a rabbit, is the only rival who appears, ever. No other product's name, logo, screenshots or trade dress, not even as a joke, not even obscured.
- **Free of real books.** Never real customer data, real company names, real amounts, real people's names. Make it up, obviously.
- **Accessible where it is read:** text at a readable size with enough contrast, no flashing, an alt-text line for any image meant for the web.

## Credit

Tell us the credit line you want: a name, a handle, a studio, a link, or "no credit". We use it as given. You can change it later by commenting on your issue.

## AI tools

Say so in the submission if any AI tool took part: generation, upscaling, in-painting, a reference it was built from. It is a disclosure, not a disqualification; we decide piece by piece, and a piece that is only a prompt is unlikely to be used.
