# SlowBooks Pro Art

Community art for [SlowBooks Pro](https://slowbookspro.com): the turtle, the app icons, the videos, the social posts, the website and marketing pieces.

**The deal, in one paragraph.** You make something, you keep it. It stays your work, under your name, and you can use it anywhere else you like. By contributing it here you let SlowBooks Pro use it, for good, in the product and in everything around the product. In return your art ships on a real product, with your name on it: in the app's About, on the website's credits, and on our social posts where the piece is the subject. No money changes hands either way. The full terms are in [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md), and contributing means you accept them whether or not you say so.

## What we are looking for

| Avenue | What it is | Guidelines |
|---|---|---|
| **Videos** | Short clips for social, walkthroughs, explainers, a release teaser | [guidelines/videos.md](guidelines/videos.md) |
| **Social media posts** | Image and short-video posts for X, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube | [guidelines/social-media.md](guidelines/social-media.md) |
| **App icons** | The icon on the Dock, the taskbar, the Start menu, the browser tab | [guidelines/app-icons.md](guidelines/app-icons.md) |
| **Website, marketing and mascot art** | Hero and feature illustrations, mascot poses and expressions, flyers, stickers, badges | [guidelines/website-marketing-mascot.md](guidelines/website-marketing-mascot.md) |

Everything is **turtle-themed**. Slow and steady, careful, honest, unhurried. The one rival who ever appears is **the Hare**, a rabbit, and the Hare is the whole of it: no other product's name, logo or look, ever. [BRAND.md](BRAND.md) has the palette, the mascot, the tone and the hard rules.

## How it works

1. **Open an issue** with the [art submission form](https://github.com/VonHoltenCodes/Slowbooks-Pro-Art/issues/new?template=art-submission.yml). Attach the files or link to them. Anyone can.
2. **We look at it.** If it is a fit, we move it to a pull request (or ask you to open one) into the right folder under `art/`. We may ask for adjustments: a size, a colour, a crop, a different pose.
3. **It gets used,** in the app, on the website, in a video, on social, or several of those. The issue gets a label saying where.
4. **You get featured:** [ARTISTS.md](ARTISTS.md) here, the About dialog in the app, the website's credits, and the posts your piece appears in.

Not every submission will be used, and we say so plainly when one is not. Keep the original files; we only need what we take.

## The rules that end things on the spot

- Vulgar, hateful, sexual or demeaning content, or anything that mocks a real person: **instant ban**, no appeal, the issue deleted.
- Someone else's work, logo, character, photo or music passed off as yours: **ban**, and the piece is removed everywhere.

The rest of the house rules are in [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## For artists, briefly

- Keep your source files (SVG, AI, PSD, project files). We prefer vector for anything that will be resized.
- Name yourself the way you want to be credited: a name, a handle, a studio, a link. Or none.
- Tell us if any AI tool had a hand in it. It is not a mark against the piece; it is something we need to know.
- Ask before you spend a weekend on something: an [idea issue](https://github.com/VonHoltenCodes/Slowbooks-Pro-Art/issues/new?template=idea.yml) takes two minutes and tells you whether we would use it.
