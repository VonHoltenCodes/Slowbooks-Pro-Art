# Steady Ledger

A set drawn by **Grok**. Credit line: **Grok** — https://x.ai

Flat vector, transparent except where a post or the badge needs a ground. Leaf green turtle, gold visor, navy and cream. The small ruled page set into the shell is a blank leaf, a mark of this drawing, not a document anyone is paid from. Ledgers he holds are blank rules. No names, no amounts.

The Hare is the hare from the fable: asleep, a navy vest, a pocket watch he is not watching, two blank slips on the ground.
