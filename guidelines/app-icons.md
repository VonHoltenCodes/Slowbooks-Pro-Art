# App icons

The icon on the Dock, the Windows taskbar and Start menu, the Linux launcher, and the browser tab. The turtle, readable at 16 pixels and handsome at 1024.

## What the icon has to do
- Read as a turtle at a glance, including at 16×16 where it is a few dozen pixels. Simplify: shell, head, visor. No ledger, no text.
- Hold its own on light and dark backgrounds, in a Dock full of other icons, and in the macOS squircle and Windows square.
- Use the palette: leaf green `#257321` for the turtle, gold `#cc9933` for the visor or an accent, navy `#003366` if a background is needed. One accent, not three.
- No text, no "SBP", no version. (A separate monogram mark is welcome under marketing art.)

## Deliverables
- **Source:** SVG, square artboard, no embedded raster.
- **macOS:** a 1024×1024 PNG, the artwork sized to Apple's template (the icon occupies about 824×824 of the 1024 canvas with the shadow margin), plus the same on a plain square for the squircle mask to crop.
- **Windows:** PNGs at 256, 128, 64, 48, 32, 24 and 16, each tuned by hand if the auto-downscale smears the face. We build the `.ico`.
- **Linux:** the 512 and 256 PNGs; the SVG does the rest.
- **Favicon:** 32, 16, and a 180×180 for touch icons.
- **A dark variant** if the icon needs one to stay readable on dark backgrounds.

## Test it
Shrink it to 16, 32 and 48 and look at it at actual size, not zoomed. Put it on white, on `#1e1e1e`, and next to a few other app icons. If you cannot tell it is a turtle at 16, it is not done.

## Open an idea issue first
An icon is the piece most likely to be asked for changes, because it is seen a thousand times a day. Show a sketch before the polish.
