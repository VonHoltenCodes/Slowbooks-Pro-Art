# Social media posts

Image or short-video posts for X, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and YouTube. One idea per post. The turtle, the ledger-paper look, navy and gold, leaf green.

## Sizes
| Use | Size |
|---|---|
| Square (Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn) | 1080×1080 |
| Portrait feed (Instagram, Facebook) | 1080×1350 |
| Landscape (X, LinkedIn, YouTube community) | 1920×1080 |
| Story / Short / Reel | 1080×1920 |
| Profile and header art | ask; it is set per platform |

PNG for anything with text or flat colour; JPEG only for photographs. Keep text and faces inside the middle 80% of the frame so no platform's crop takes them.

## Writing on a post
- Short. A headline and at most one line under it.
- True. Every claim about the product must be something it does today. If you are not sure, ask.
- Say "free" and "source-available". Never "open source".
- The only link is slowbookspro.com.
- Supply **alt text** for the image in the submission: one sentence saying what is in it.
- No version strings or build numbers. "2.30" when the post is about a release is fine.

## Themes that work
- The turtle doing bookkeeping things calmly: reconciling, filing, finishing the month with time to spare.
- The Hare rushing, losing receipts, missing the deadline; the turtle already done. Never mean, never a real product.
- A real feature, one screen, one sentence, made-up data.
- Seasons: year end, tax time, a new quarter, a long weekend. No religious or political holidays.
- Release art: the turtle in a new pose per release, the release number, one line about what shipped.

## Do not
- Name, show or hint at any real product, however obscured.
- Use real customer names, companies, amounts or people.
- Use someone else's photo, font, meme template or character.
- Put the turtle on a document a customer or tax authority would receive.
