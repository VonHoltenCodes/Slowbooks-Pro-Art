# Videos

What we use: 15 to 60 second clips for social; 1 to 3 minute walkthroughs of one thing; a release teaser now and then. Always the turtle somewhere, even if only the icon.

## Before you start
Open an idea issue with a one-line premise and, if it is a walkthrough, which screen. We will confirm the product does what the video shows. A video that shows the app doing something it does not do is unusable, however good it looks.

## Formats
- **Landscape 16:9** at 1920×1080 or better for YouTube, the website and X.
- **Portrait 9:16** at 1080×1920 for Shorts, Reels and Stories, when the piece suits it. The same clip cut both ways is ideal.
- MP4, H.264, AAC. 30 or 60 fps. Deliver the master at full quality; we make the small ones.
- A thumbnail (1280×720 PNG) for anything over 30 seconds.

## Content
- **Screen recordings:** the current release only, on a made-up company with obviously fake names and amounts. Never a real set of books. Never another product's screen, not even blurred.
- **Captions:** burned in or as an .srt. Many people watch with the sound off.
- **Music and voice:** your own, licensed for our use, or public domain. Say which in the submission. No library tracks whose licence does not cover a company's social posts.
- **People:** anyone on camera or on the mic has agreed to appear in SlowBooks Pro's marketing. Say so in the submission.
- **Length:** cut it in half, then look again.
- **No flashing** faster than three times a second, anywhere.

## The Hare in video
The Hare can race, trip, forget the receipts, miss the deadline. The Hare cannot be a logo, a colour scheme, a slogan or a screenshot that means a real product. Keep the fable the fable.

## Delivery
Files up to 100 MB in a pull request; larger ones as an unlisted YouTube link or a shared folder in the issue. Project files (Premiere, Resolve, Blender, After Effects) are welcome if you are willing; we will not alter your edit without asking.
