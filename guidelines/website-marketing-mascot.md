# Website, marketing and mascot art

Illustrations for slowbookspro.com, flyers and handouts, stickers and badges, and the turtle itself in new poses and expressions.

## Mascot art
- Keep the turtle recognisable: green shell, visor, upright, the same proportions as the reference. New poses and expressions are welcome; a new character design is a conversation first.
- Poses we can always use: pointing at something, peeking in from an edge, celebrating, thinking, waving, carrying a box of receipts, wearing the construction hard hat, holding a coffee at the end of the month.
- Expressions: happy, focused, smug, surprised, wink, and the one you think is missing.
- **Vector** (SVG) so it can be resized and recoloured. If you work in raster, 4000 pixels on the long side with transparency.
- Separate the parts on layers or groups (shell, head, visor, limbs, prop) so a pose can be animated later.
- The Hare as a character: fast, flashy, a bit vain, never cruel, never a real product.

## Website art
- Hero illustrations around 2400×1200, feature spots around 800×800, both with transparent backgrounds so they sit on the ledger-paper cream.
- Navy, gold and leaf green on cream. Flat or lightly shaded; no heavy gradients, no drop shadows, no photoreal.
- Screens of the app inside an illustration use made-up data and the current release.
- Every image gets one line of alt text in the submission.

## Marketing and print
- Flyers and one-pagers: US Letter and A4, 300 dpi, CMYK-safe colours (we convert), bleed 3 mm.
- Stickers and badges: round 25 mm and 50 mm, square 50 mm, die-cut turtle. Vector only.
- Slides: 1920×1080, the title in Inter Bold, the turtle in the corner, not the middle.
- No version strings or build numbers; "SlowBooks Pro" and, when it matters, the release number.

## Claims
Anything a flyer or a page says the product does must be true of it as shipped. Open an idea issue with the words you want to use and we will confirm or correct them.
